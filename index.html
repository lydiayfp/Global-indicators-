Three data providers, three free keys. Real index levels and a real credit spread aren't available from a single free source, so this now calls: Alpha Vantage (Treasury curve, CPI, Brent, spot gold), Twelve Data (true S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite index levels), and FRED (ICE BofA High Yield credit spread). Each has its own rate limit — Alpha Vantage is the tight one (25/day, 5/min); Twelve Data and FRED are generous.
Heads up on FRED specifically: the St. Louis Fed's API generally does not allow direct browser calls (no CORS headers) — it's built for server-side use. It may simply fail here regardless of your key. If so, the credit-spread card will show that error, and your workaround is checking the FRED page directly or opening this file outside the chat sandbox in your own browser, which sometimes clears CORS issues that are specific to this environment (though FRED's lack of CORS support is on their end, not just the sandbox).
